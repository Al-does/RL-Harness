How to read this study

Each point is a three-coordinate block of the full belief, wnηn. Its coordinate sum is that component's posterior probability. The triangular plane has vertices (w,0,0), (0,w,0), (0,0,w); it shrinks toward the origin as w approaches zero. Sweeping w from zero to one fills a tetrahedral envelope. Component coordinate indices appear on the axes.

Sequence mode shows a growing trajectory, a large point at the current position, and its weighted plane. Probe planes use the raw decoded component mass, which may be negative or exceed one. Predictions are never clipped or renormalized. All panels share axes expanded to include plotted predictions.

Point and plane colors blend red, green, and blue by local state composition. Brightness increases with the square root of component weight. For out-of-simplex predictions, only the color calculation uses positive coordinates and bounded weight. Checkpoint outlines, trails, and marker shapes retain checkpoint identity.

High probe fit measures linear accessibility; it does not establish causal use or uniquely identify internal computations. Metrics use all supplied held-out positions, not just the displayed sample. Read the experiment reports for provenance, timing, sampling, warmup, controls, uncertainty and limitations.
